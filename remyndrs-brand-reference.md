## What Remyndrs Is

Remyndrs is an AI-powered memory assistant that works entirely through text message. Users text anything they want to remember — a reminder, a list item, a fact — and Remyndrs handles it automatically. No app to download. No account to log into. No learning curve.

**The three core features:**
- **Reminders** — Set a time-based reminder by texting it naturally ("Remind me at 3pm to pick up the kids")
- **Lists** — Add to and retrieve lists by text ("Add goldfish crackers to my grocery list" / "What's on my grocery list?")
- **Memory / Recall** — Save any piece of information and ask for it later ("Remember my WiFi password is BlueSky42" / "What's my WiFi password?")

**How to start:** Text GO (parents page) or Hello (main page) to +1 (855) 552-1950

**Pricing:** Free tier available forever. 14-day Premium trial included. Premium is $8.99/month.

## Who It's For

The primary advertising audience is **parents** — specifically busy parents managing school schedules, grocery lists, kids' activities, and medication reminders. This is where ad spend is focused and where conversion has been strongest.

Secondary audiences (present on the main homepage):
- People with ADHD or scattered focus ("100 tabs open")
- Professionals tracking follow-ups and deadlines
- Anyone who wants something simple with no app to manage

**Important:** When writing for the parents page or Facebook ads, write specifically for parents. Don't try to speak to everyone at once — the specificity is what makes the copy land.

## Voice & Tone

**Remyndrs sounds like:**
- A practical, warm friend who gets how chaotic life is
- Direct and plain-spoken — no jargon, no fluff
- Confident without being salesy
- Empathetic to real parent chaos moments

**Remyndrs does NOT sound like:**
- A tech company ("leverage," "seamless," "robust," "powerful AI")
- Generic SaaS ("streamline your workflow," "optimize your productivity")
- Overly cute or try-hard
- Formal or corporate

**Writing rules:**
- Write like you'd text a friend, not pitch a product
- Lead with the problem or the moment, not the feature
- Specific beats generic every time ("picture day" beats "school events")
- Short sentences. Real words. No filler.

## Facebook Ad Strategy

**Current setup:** $7/day, video ad format, targeting parents

**What's working:** The video hook concept — leading with a specific, relatable parent moment before introducing the product. The class party list hook ("Your kid's teacher just texted the class party list. Now what?") is the current approved hook.

**Key principle:** The Facebook ad hook and the landing page H1 must match. When you change the ad, update the page headline to mirror it. Message mismatch between ad and landing page causes immediate bounce.

**Hook angles that test well:**
- Specific chaotic parent moment ("teacher just texted," "forgot picture day")
- First-person admission ("I forgot to RSVP again — so I built something")
- Outcome-focused ("Never miss another school deadline")

**Hook angles to avoid:**
- Feature-first ("Reminders, Lists, and Memories by text!")
- Generic benefit ("The easiest reminder app")
- Anything that requires explaining what the product is before creating interest

## Landing Page Principles

**The ad and the H1 must match** — always. The visitor's brain expects continuity from the ad they clicked.

**Mobile first** — 94% of traffic is mobile. Every copy and UX decision should be evaluated on a phone screen first.

**Paid traffic converts at 13%; direct at 26%** — people who already know Remyndrs convert at 2x the rate. The gap is a trust/clarity problem on the landing page, not an audience problem.

**The SMS flow has a hidden drop-off point** — when users tap the mobile CTA, their Messages app opens pre-filled. Many don't realize they need to hit Send. Always include the micro-copy: "✓ Your Messages app will open — just hit Send!"

**Pricing creates confusion if shown too early** — the pricing strip ("Free to start · 14-day Premium trial · Then $8.99/mo") should appear after "How It Works," not immediately after the hero CTA. Showing price before value = drop-off.

**The interactive demo is below the fold on mobile** — most paid traffic users never see it. Include a teaser above the CTA: "See how it works ↓ (30 seconds)"
